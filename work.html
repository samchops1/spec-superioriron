The work

From the drawing to the install.

We can help visualize and design unique projects for almost any situation. Colorful, eclectic work — or the rail that has to hold. Reach out for the next metal project.

Design

We can help visualize and design unique projects for almost any situation. We love inspiring people and creating colorful and eclectic works.

Metal fabrication

A variety of metal fabrication services, including commissioned sculptures. We use recycled materials to fabricate onsite.

Architectural

We got our start making security doors, handrails, and fences. We are happy to help with architectural embellishments to make your building pop. We also create custom signs.

Public art

We have successfully completed a number of public art projects around the city and county of Denver and beyond. We are well versed in the public art process and welcome collaboration.

Installation

We can work from design, through fabrication and installation, on a variety of different kinds of projects.

Metal recycling

We recycle metal from various uses and put it to work in our artistic sculptures. Reach out if you have something you’d like to donate to our projects.
